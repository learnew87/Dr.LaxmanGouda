Executive Summary

Bridging Breakthrough Science to Industrial Clean Energy

I am a clean-tech researcher, entrepreneur, and Ramanujan Fellow at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, and Co-Founder & CEO of ABX3PV Private Limited.

My scientific trajectory centers on transitioning Hybrid Perovskite solar cells from laboratory scale to industrial reality. We develop scalable continuous manufacturing techniques including Sheet-to-Sheet and Roll-to-Roll fabrication on flexible and rigid substrates.

Beyond photovoltaics, my work advances solar-fuel interfaces: photo-electrosynthesis of green Hydrogen and direct carbon dioxide (CO2) capture stored into chemical bonds for next-generation renewable energy storage.

  • ☀️ Industrial-scale Perovskite module continuous manufacturing
  • 🌱 Photo-electrosynthesis of Hydrogen & Direct CO2 capture
  • 🔬 Combinatorial high-throughput screening & advanced nano-optics
  • 🌍 International research background: Israel, Italy, Switzerland, Spain, UK, & India
